Writeups are detailed records of completed security rooms and challenges from
hands-on CTF and learning platforms. They document the route from the first
observation to a solution: the tools used, assumptions that did not hold up,
the reasoning behind each change in direction, and what the exercise taught me.

The objective is to leave a clear path I can revisit and, when useful, another
learner can follow. Entries are organized by the platform that hosted the
original room. Only platforms with published work appear here.
